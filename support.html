Frequently asked questions

How do I add music?

Open any audio file from the Files app and choose Share → Aera, or use Import inside the app. Your library stays empty until you add tracks.

Where are my files stored?

On your device only — in Aera's private storage and under On My iPhone → Aera in the Files app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I set custom artwork for a track?

Edit a track, tap the artwork, then choose a photo from your library, take a new photo, paste an image, or search the web.

Does Aera work offline?

Yes. Aera has no accounts and no server — it works entirely offline.

How do I delete a track?

Swipe a track in your library and remove it. Removing a track from All Tracks deletes the file and its record; removing it from another playlist only unlists it there.
